# Personal wallet <-> company connection

## Person starts the connection

1. Personal mode: 「新しい勤務先と接続」.
2. Search companies by company name/address/corporate number/SKO company ID.
3. Select the company and send an employment connection request.
4. Company administrator approves.
5. Person authenticates and reviews the personal data to share.
6. Company workspace is added; personal workspace remains.

## Company starts the connection

1. Employee registration: 「既にSKOを利用している人を招待」.
2. Enter the person's personal SKO ID.
3. Send invitation. Do not reveal wallet contents from the ID lookup.
4. Person receives the invitation, authenticates, reviews requested personal data and approves.
5. Company administrator completes/accepts the membership.
6. Company workspace is added; personal workspace remains.

## Privacy

- Company search lists companies only, never people.
- Personal SKO ID is an addressing key, not an authentication secret.
- Knowing a personal SKO ID never grants access to profile, qualifications or documents.
- Transferable personal data requires explicit person approval.
- Company-owned payroll/invoice/approval records never transfer to a new employer.
